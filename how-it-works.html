How SafeRide works

A clear four-step ride journey.

01

Choose pickup

Enter your pickup location and destination. The booking screen can estimate a route and fare.

02

Review ride

Choose a ride type and review the estimated fare and trip details.

03

Check driver

Review the available driver's name, rating, vehicle and estimated arrival.

04

Track and rate

Follow trip status, use the safety controls when needed, then rate the completed ride.
