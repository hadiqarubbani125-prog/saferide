SAFETY CENTER

Safety features for every trip.

These are product safeguards and guidance for the SafeRide concept. Emergency services should always be contacted directly when appropriate.

Driver information

Show driver name, photo, rating, vehicle details and ETA before pickup.

Route visibility

Keep pickup, destination and route visible so riders can confirm the trip they requested.

Trip sharing

A production version should let riders share live trip details with a trusted contact.

SOS

Provide a clear emergency control and local emergency guidance. Do not rely on the website alone for emergency response.

Ratings and reports

Allow riders to rate trips and report safety concerns for follow-up.

Account protection

Production authentication should use secure sessions, protected passwords and server-side validation.

Production note: this static website is a front-end demo. Real authentication, dispatch, payments, databases, verified driver onboarding and emergency integrations require a secure backend.
